Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Kulki ("the App") is developed and maintained by Mateusz Pietryga ("the Developer") as free, open-source software.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no internet access requirement and does not connect to any server, analytics service, or third party.

Data Collection

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to any external service

Local Data Storage

The App stores a small configuration and score file on your device, in the app's private internal storage area. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted over the network
  • Is not accessible to the Developer or any third party
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking libraries.

Children's Privacy

Since the App collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Permissions

The App requests no runtime permissions beyond standard storage access required to read/write its own local save file (where applicable to the platform).

Open Source

The App is open-source software. Its full source code is available for inspection at:
https://github.com/jockx-net/kulki

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be published at the same URL where this policy is hosted, with an updated "Last updated" date.
